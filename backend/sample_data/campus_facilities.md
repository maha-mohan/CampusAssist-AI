# Campus Facilities (SAMPLE DATA)

ABC Engineering College is on a 60-acre green campus on Avinashi Road, Coimbatore, with academic blocks, hostels, a sports complex, and student amenities.

## Canteen
Open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian items are available.

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Idli (2) with sambar | ₹25 |
| Dosa | ₹35 |
| Veg meals | ₹60 |
| Chicken biryani | ₹110 |
| Fried rice with gravy | ₹80 |
| Tea / coffee | ₹10 / ₹15 |
| Fruit juice | ₹30 |
| Samosa / cutlet | ₹15 |

A separate juice and snack counter is in the library block. Payment by UPI is accepted.

## Sports and Fitness
- Cricket ground, football ground, volleyball, basketball and tennis courts
- Indoor stadium for badminton, table tennis, chess and carrom
- Athletic track (400 m)
- Gym open 6:00 AM – 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM
- Sports coaches for cricket, football, volleyball and athletics. Students can join the college teams by trials in August.

## Medical Centre
A medical room is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, with a nurse and a visiting doctor on Tuesdays and Fridays. Serious cases are taken to the tie-up hospital in the city (10 km), and the college ambulance is available 24 hours. All students are covered by a group medical insurance policy.

## Bank and ATM
A bank branch and an ATM are inside the campus. The branch works from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM on weekdays.

## Other Facilities
- **Wi-Fi:** available in all academic blocks, the library and the hostels.
- **Auditorium:** air-conditioned, with 800 seats, used for seminars, annual day and guest lectures.
- **Seminar halls:** 6 halls with projection facilities, for department events.
- **Stationery and photocopy shop:** near the canteen, open 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM.
- **Drinking water:** RO purified water points on every floor.
- **Parking:** separate parking areas for two-wheelers, cars and buses. A parking sticker (₹200 per year) is needed for student vehicles.
- **Post office / courier:** collection point at the College Office.
- **Prayer / meditation room:** open throughout the working hours.
- **Facilities for differently-abled students:** ramps, lifts in all blocks and a reserved seating area.

## Security
The campus has CCTV cameras and security guards round the clock. Visitors must register at the main gate.

## Contact
College Office: office@college.edu. Facility issues can be raised as a ticket under the Other category.
