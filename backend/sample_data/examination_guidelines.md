# Examination Guidelines (SAMPLE DATA)

These guidelines apply to all undergraduate and postgraduate students. The Examination Cell (Controller of Examinations office) conducts all semester, arrear and revaluation processes in line with Anna University regulations.

## Semester Examinations
- End-semester examinations for odd semesters are held in November and December.
- End-semester examinations for even semesters are held in April and May.
- The detailed timetable is published on the college website and notice boards at least three weeks before the exams.
- Internal assessments (two per subject each semester) are held during the semester. Marks are displayed on the student ERP portal.

## Exam Registration
Students register for end-semester examinations on the student ERP portal during the registration window announced in the timetable. Registration needs the exam fee of ₹2,500 per semester to be paid. A late registration fee of ₹250 applies if registration is done within 10 days after the window closes.

## Hall Ticket
- Hall tickets can be downloaded from the student ERP portal one week before the examination.
- Students must have at least 75% attendance and must have paid all fees to receive the hall ticket.
- Students with attendance between 65% and 74% can apply for condonation through the Examination Cell with a medical or other valid reason.
- If the hall ticket is not available or has incorrect details, contact the Examination Cell with your register number. Bring a photo ID to the exam hall.

## Exam Conduct
- Reach the exam hall 15 minutes before the start time. Late entry is not allowed after the first 30 minutes.
- Mobile phones, smart watches and written notes are not allowed in the hall.
- Malpractice, including copying, impersonation or possession of unauthorised material, leads to cancellation of the paper and disciplinary action as per college rules.

## Passing Criteria
- A student passes a subject with a minimum of 50% in the combined internal and external marks, and at least 45% in the external (end-semester) examination.
- Grades are awarded on a 10-point scale. Grade O is for 91 and above, A+ for 81 to 90, A for 71 to 80, B+ for 61 to 70, B for 56 to 60, C for 50 to 55, and U for below 50 (fail).

## Results and Revaluation
- Results are published on the student portal within 30 days after the last examination.
- Students can apply for revaluation within 7 days of the result announcement. The revaluation fee is ₹500 per paper.
- Students can apply for photocopy of the answer script within 7 days of the result. The photocopy fee is ₹300 per paper.
- Revaluation results are published within 15 working days of the application.

## Arrear Exams
Arrear (supplementary) examinations are conducted along with the regular semester examinations. Students must register for arrear papers on the ERP portal during the exam registration window. The arrear exam fee is ₹500 per paper, in addition to the semester exam fee.

## Mark Sheets, Transcripts and Degree Certificates
- Provisional mark sheets are issued by the Examination Cell after the results are published.
- Consolidated mark sheets are issued after the final semester results and clearance of all arrears.
- The degree certificate is issued within 60 days after the final result, once the student has cleared all dues and returned the library books.

## Grievances
Grievances about results, attendance or hall tickets can be raised as a ticket under the Examination category. Each grievance is acknowledged within 2 working days and resolved within 15 working days.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Can I write the exam if my fee is pending?** No. The hall ticket is blocked until all fees are paid.
- **What if I miss my exam for a medical reason?** Submit the medical certificate to the Examination Cell within 7 days. Permission for a special exam is decided case by case.

Contact the Examination Cell with your register number at coe@college.edu.
