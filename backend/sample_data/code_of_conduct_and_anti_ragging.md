# Code of Conduct and Anti-Ragging Policy (SAMPLE DATA)

## General Conduct
- Students must carry the college identity card at all times and show it at the main gate and to staff on request.
- Respect for teachers, staff, fellow students and visitors is expected at all times.
- Mobile phones must be on silent in classrooms, labs and the library.
- Smoking, alcohol, tobacco and drugs are strictly prohibited on the campus and in the hostels.
- Damage to college property must be paid for, and the student may be penalised.
- Students must not take part in political activities or demonstrations on the campus.

## Dress Code
- Students must wear formal or smart-casual clothes. Round-neck T-shirts with slogans, torn jeans, shorts and slippers are not allowed in classes.
- Department uniform or lab coats are mandatory in laboratories and workshops.
- On Fridays, students may wear college-branded T-shirts or traditional dress.

## Anti-Ragging Policy
Ragging in any form is a criminal offence and is **banned** on the campus, in the hostels, buses and any place outside the campus, as per the directions of the Supreme Court of India and the UGC regulations.

Ragging includes teasing, humiliating, forcing a student to do anything against their will, or causing physical or mental harm.

**Penalties** (as decided by the Anti-Ragging Committee):
- Cancellation of admission.
- Suspension or expulsion from the college and the hostel.
- Withholding of results, scholarships and other benefits.
- A police complaint and criminal proceedings.

Every student and parent signs an anti-ragging undertaking at admission.

## How to Report Ragging
- Anti-ragging helpline (24x7): +91 422 000 0099
- Email: antiragging@college.edu
- Any member of the Anti-Ragging Committee or the hostel warden.
- The national anti-ragging helpline 1800-180-5522 (toll free).

Complaints are kept confidential, and action is taken within 24 hours.

## Disciplinary Process
1. A written complaint or a report is received by the Dean (Student Affairs).
2. The Discipline Committee examines the case, and the student gets a chance to explain.
3. The committee recommends the action: warning, fine, suspension or expulsion.
4. The Principal's decision is final. An appeal can be made to the Management within 15 days.

## Student Grievances
Students can raise grievances about teaching, facilities or staff behaviour with the Grievance Redressal Cell at grievance@college.edu or as a ticket under the Other category. Each grievance is acknowledged within 2 working days and resolved within 15 working days.

## Internal Complaints Committee
Complaints of harassment or discrimination are handled by the Internal Complaints Committee (icc@college.edu), in line with the law. All complaints are handled with strict confidentiality.
