## National Service Scheme (NSS), NCC and YRC
- **NSS:** community service, blood donation camps and village adoption. Students earn extra-curricular credit.
- **NCC:** Army wing unit with weekly parade on Saturday mornings.
- **Youth Red Cross (YRC):** first aid training and health awareness camps.

## Inter-College Participation
Students who represent the college at inter-college events get OD leave. Apply for OD at least 2 days before the event with the staff in charge.

## Starting a New Club
A group of at least 15 students and a faculty coordinator can propose a new club to the Dean (Student Affairs).
