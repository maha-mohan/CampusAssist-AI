# Library Rules and Services (SAMPLE DATA)

The Central Library holds textbooks, reference books, project reports and back-volume journals for every department. Students get access to the library with their college identity card. The tuition fee already includes library fees.

## Library Timings
- Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Reading room during the examination period: open until 8:00 PM on weekdays, with prior notice on the notice board.
- Digital journal access is available 24x7 from the campus network. Off-campus access uses the library login on the student ERP portal.
- The library is closed on Sundays and government holidays.

## Borrowing Rules
| Member | Books allowed at a time | Loan period |
|---|---|---|
| Undergraduate students | 3 books | 14 days |
| Postgraduate students | 5 books | 21 days |
| Faculty and staff | 10 books | 30 days |

- Books can be renewed once, online or at the counter, if no other student has reserved them.
- Reference books, project reports and journals are for reading inside the library only.

## Fines
- A fine of ₹2 per book per day applies for late return of books.
- A book lost or damaged must be replaced with the same title and edition, or paid for at the current price set by the Librarian.
- Fines and dues must be cleared before the hall ticket is issued, and before the caution deposit or the degree certificate is released.

## Other Services
- Book bank: textbooks for economically weaker students, issued for one semester on request to the Librarian.
- Plagiarism check of project reports and thesis drafts, on request, before submission.
- Inter-library borrowing with partner colleges, arranged through the Librarian.

## Rules Inside the Library
- Keep mobile phones on silent. Calls must be taken outside the reading room.
- Food and drinks are not allowed inside the library.
- Bags must be kept at the bag counter at the entrance.

## Contact
Central Library: library@college.edu. Book requests, renewals and fine queries can also be raised as a ticket under the Library category.
