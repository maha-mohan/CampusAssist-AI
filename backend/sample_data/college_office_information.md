# College Office Services and Certificates (SAMPLE DATA)

The College Office is the first point of contact for general enquiries, certificates and documents issued by the college. It is located at the main administrative block on the Avinashi Road campus, about 8 km from Coimbatore Junction railway station.

## Office Hours
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. The office is closed on Sundays and government holidays.

## Certificates Issued
| Certificate | Fee | Processing time |
|---|---|---|
| Bonafide certificate | ₹50 | Within 2 working days |
| Fee-paid certificate | ₹50 | Within 2 working days |
| Study certificate | ₹100 | Within 3 working days |
| Transfer certificate (for students leaving the college) | ₹200 | Within 7 working days |
| Conduct certificate | ₹50 | Within 2 working days |

To request a certificate:
1. Fill in the certificate request form at the College Office, or download it from the student ERP portal.
2. Attach a copy of your college identity card.
3. Pay the certificate fee at the Accounts Office, and bring the receipt to the College Office.
4. Collect the certificate from the College Office on the stated date. Certificates are not sent by post unless requested in writing and paid for.

## Identity Cards
- The college identity card is issued during the first week of the first semester, after the fee payment is confirmed.
- A lost identity card must be reported to the College Office at once. A duplicate card is issued for ₹100 after a written request and a police complaint copy, if the card was lost outside the campus.

## Student Records
- Name, address, mobile number and parent details can be changed on the student ERP portal. Name changes need a copy of the Aadhaar card or the school transfer certificate.
- Requests to correct a date of birth are considered only with the Class 10 mark sheet and a written application.

## Visitors and Appointments
Parents and visitors can meet the Principal's office or the Dean through an appointment made at the College Office. Visitors must sign the visitor register at the main gate and wear a visitor pass inside the campus.

## Complaints and Suggestions
Complaints and suggestions can be raised in person at the College Office, by email at office@college.edu, or as a ticket under the Other category. Certificate queries can be raised under the Certificates category. Each complaint is acknowledged within 2 working days.

## Contact
College Office: office@college.edu, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM.
