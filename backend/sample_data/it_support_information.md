# IT Support and Campus Network Policy (SAMPLE DATA)

The IT Support team looks after the campus Wi-Fi, the student ERP portal, the placement and admission portals, college email accounts and the computer labs. Students can get help in person at the IT Support desk, by email or through a ticket from the helpdesk chat.

## Support Desk Hours
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Urgent network outages are attended to outside these hours on a best-effort basis.

## Student Accounts
- Each student gets a college email account and an ERP portal login on admission. The login is created within 3 working days after the admission fee is paid.
- Forgotten ERP passwords are reset at the IT Support desk after the student shows the college identity card. Passwords are never shared over phone or chat.
- Email accounts and ERP logins are deactivated 60 days after the student leaves the college, unless the student has an active project or a pending degree.

## Campus Wi-Fi
- Wi-Fi is available in classrooms, the library, hostels and the canteen.
- Log in with the ERP username and password. Each student device is limited to 2 active sessions at a time.
- Fair-usage limits apply. Torrent downloads and streaming of large video files are blocked during class hours.

## Computer Labs
- Lab timings are displayed outside each lab. Labs are open for students from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days, and for project work with the faculty's permission in the evening.
- Students must not install unlicensed software or change the lab system settings.
- Damage to lab systems must be reported to the lab in-charge on the same day.

## Acceptable Use
- Use the network only for academic work, placement and college administration.
- Do not share your login with anyone, and do not try to access another person's account or records.
- Report phishing emails or suspicious messages to it@college.edu at once.

## Reporting a Problem
1. Note the room, the device, and the exact error message.
2. Raise a ticket from the helpdesk chat under the Technical category, or email it@college.edu.
3. IT Support acknowledges each problem within 2 working days. Wi-Fi and portal outages are resolved as quickly as possible, and the status can be checked with the ticket ID.

## Contact
IT Support: it@college.edu. Technical issues with the admission portal, ERP or placement portal can also be raised as a ticket under the Technical category.
