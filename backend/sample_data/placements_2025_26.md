## Training and Preparation
- **Aptitude training:** weekly sessions from the second year, covering quantitative aptitude and logical reasoning.
- **Technical training:** coding labs in Java, Python and SQL, with mock technical interviews every semester.
- **Soft-skills workshops:** communication, group discussion and resume-writing workshops each semester.
- **Mock interviews:** conducted by alumni and industry volunteers twice a year before the placement season.
- **Internships:** students can intern at partner companies from the summer after the second year. Internship offers are tracked in the placement portal.

## Eligibility for Placements
Students need a CGPA of 6.5 or above with no standing arrears to register with the placement cell. Final-year students should register on the placement portal before 30 June of their final year. Students who take a break in studies must inform the placement cell before registering.

## Placement Process
1. Register on the placement portal and complete the profile, including resume and certificates.
2. Attend the pre-placement talk held by the company, usually one to two weeks before the drive.
3. Take the online test, which usually covers aptitude, technical questions and coding.
4. Appear for the technical and HR interview rounds.
5. Accept or decline the offer letter through the placement portal. Accepting an offer and then declining it without a valid reason may affect eligibility for later drives.

## Off-Campus Offers
Students who receive an off-campus offer must inform the placement cell within 3 working days. Offers are recorded in the placement portal and counted in the placement report.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Can I register if I have one arrear?** No. Students need no standing arrears to register.
- **Do MBA students get placed through the same cell?** Yes. MBA students register on the same placement portal, with separate drives for finance and analytics roles.
