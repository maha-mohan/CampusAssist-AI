# Transport Information 2026-27 (SAMPLE DATA)

The college runs its own bus service for students and staff. College buses run on 25 routes across Coimbatore, Tiruppur and Pollachi. Each bus has a driver, a conductor and a GPS tracker, and the Transport Office maintains the route list and timings.

## Bus Fee 2026-27
| Distance from campus | Annual bus fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 10 km | ₹12,000 |
| 10 km to 20 km | ₹16,000 |
| Above 20 km | ₹20,000 |

The bus fee is separate from the tuition fee. It is payable in two instalments, the same schedule as the tuition fee. Bus pass fees are not refunded for days missed during the year.

## How to Get a Bus Pass
1. Check the route and the stop nearest to your home on the route list at the Transport Office or on the notice board.
2. Fill in the bus pass application form with your register number, name, mobile number and the stop you will board from.
3. Attach one passport-size photograph and a copy of your college identity card.
4. Pay the bus fee at the Accounts Office and submit the receipt at the Transport Office.
5. Collect the bus pass from the Transport Office. Passes are issued within 3 working days of the fee payment.

## Timings and Rules
- Buses leave the campus for the morning run at 7:45 AM and return on the evening run at 4:45 PM, Monday to Saturday.
- Students must board only at their assigned stop, and must show the bus pass to the conductor.
- Lost bus passes are replaced for a fee of ₹200 after a written request to the Transport Office.
- Students must not stand in the aisle, lean out of the windows or disturb the driver while the bus is moving.

## Changing Route or Stop
A change of route or stop is allowed once in a semester, if seats are available on the new route. Submit the request at the Transport Office with the new address proof.

## Transport Office Hours
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. The office is closed on Sundays and government holidays.

## Contact
Transport Office: transport@college.edu. Bus pass and route queries can also be raised as a ticket under the Transport category.
