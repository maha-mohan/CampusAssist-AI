# Admission Notification 2026-27 (SAMPLE DATA)

ABC Engineering College, Coimbatore invites applications for B.E. / B.Tech first-year admission for the academic year 2026-27. The college is affiliated to Anna University, Chennai and approved by AICTE. Admissions are offered through two routes: the government quota (TNEA counselling) and the management quota (direct admission by the college).

## Important Dates
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Online application opens | 15 May 2026 |
| Last date for B.E./B.Tech admission application | 31 July 2026 |
| Management quota counselling | 5 August 2026 to 10 August 2026 |
| Last date for lateral entry applications | 20 August 2026 |
| Commencement of classes for first year | 18 August 2026 |

## Seats Available
First-year intake for 2026-27 is 720 seats across seven undergraduate programmes: B.E. Computer Science and Engineering (180), B.Tech Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (120), B.Tech Information Technology (120), B.E. Electronics and Communication Engineering (120), B.E. Electrical and Electronics Engineering (60), B.E. Mechanical Engineering (60) and B.E. Civil Engineering (60). Seat allotment in the government quota follows TNEA rules. Management quota seats are filled on a first-come, first-served basis after counselling.

## Eligibility
Candidates must have passed Higher Secondary (Class 12) with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. The minimum aggregate in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry is:
- 45% for the general category
- 40% for BC / MBC candidates
- 35% for SC / ST candidates

Candidates appearing in Class 12 supplementary examinations are not eligible to apply for the management quota until their results are declared.

## How to Apply
1. Create an account on the Admission Portal on the college website using a valid mobile number and email address.
2. Fill in the application form with personal details, parent details, Class 10 and Class 12 marks, and the programme preferences in order.
3. Upload scanned copies of the Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets, the transfer certificate, the community certificate (for BC, MBC, SC or ST candidates) and a passport-size photograph. Each file must be a PDF or JPG under 2 MB.
4. Pay the application fee of ₹1,000 online by debit card, credit card, UPI or net banking. The application fee is non-refundable.
5. Download the application acknowledgement. Keep the application number for all future correspondence.

## Documents Required at Admission
Bring the original documents and two self-attested photocopies of each to the Admissions Office on the day of counselling:
- Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets and pass certificates
- Transfer certificate from the last school attended
- Community certificate (BC, MBC, SC or ST candidates)
- Aadhaar card
- Passport-size photographs (six copies)
- Income certificate, if the candidate is applying for a fee concession

## Management Quota Counselling
Counselling is held from 5 August to 10 August 2026 in the Admissions Office from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Shortlisted candidates receive an allotment letter by email and SMS, with the reporting date and time. Candidates who do not report on the allotted date lose their seat. Parents may accompany the candidate.

## Government Quota Admission
Government quota seats are filled through TNEA counselling conducted by the Government of Tamil Nadu. Candidates who receive a college allotment through TNEA must report to the Admissions Office with the allotment order and the documents listed above. The college does not charge the application fee for government quota candidates.

## Lateral Entry
Diploma holders can apply for direct second-year (lateral entry) admission. The diploma must be in a relevant engineering discipline from an institution approved by AICTE or the State Board of Technical Education, Tamil Nadu. The last date for lateral entry applications is 20 August 2026. Lateral entry candidates must bring their diploma mark sheets and the completion certificate.

## Refund Policy for Admission Fees
- Application fee: non-refundable.
- Admission fee and caution deposit: refundable only if the candidate withdraws before the first day of classes. Refund requests are processed within 30 working days through the Accounts Office.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Can I apply without a Class 12 mathematics mark?** No. Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry must all be part of the Class 12 subjects.
- **Is there a hostel for first-year students?** Yes. Hostel forms are issued after the college admission is confirmed. See the Hostel Information document for details.
- **Can I change my programme after admission?** Programme change requests are considered once, at the end of the first semester, subject to seat availability and the department's approval.
