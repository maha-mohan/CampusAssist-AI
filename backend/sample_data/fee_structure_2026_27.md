# Fee Structure 2026-27 (SAMPLE DATA)

The fees below apply to students admitted in the academic year 2026-27 under the management quota. Government quota fees are as fixed by the Fee Regulatory Committee, Government of Tamil Nadu. Fees are shown in Indian rupees (₹).

The tuition fee includes library and laboratory fees. It does not include hostel, transport, exam or other charges listed below.

## One-Time and Per-Semester Charges
| Charge | Amount | When payable |
|---|---|---|
| Admission fee (one-time) | ₹5,000 | At admission |
| Caution deposit (refundable) | ₹5,000 | At admission |
| Exam fee | ₹2,500 per semester | Before exam registration each semester |
| Student activity fee | ₹1,000 per year | With the first instalment |

## Payment Schedule
The annual tuition fee can be paid in two instalments:
- **First instalment (60%):** at admission, before the first day of classes.
- **Second instalment (40%):** before 15 December 2026.

Students who pay the full annual fee at admission receive a 2% discount on the tuition fee.

## Payment Methods
- Online through the college fee portal (debit card, credit card, UPI or net banking).
- NEFT to the college bank account. Quote the student's register number and name in the transfer reference.
- Demand draft drawn in favour of "ABC Engineering College", payable at Coimbatore.
- Cash is not accepted for fee payments.

## Late Payment
A late fee of ₹100 per day applies after the due date. Fees more than 60 days overdue lead to suspension of the exam registration and the hall ticket until the dues are cleared.

## Fee Receipts and Certificates
- Receipts for online payments are available on the fee portal immediately after payment.
- For NEFT or demand draft payments, submit the bank reference at the Accounts Office. The receipt is issued within 2 working days.
- Fee-paid certificates and bonafide certificates are issued by the College Office within 2 working days of the request.

## Refund of Caution Deposit
The caution deposit is refunded after the student completes the programme or leaves the college, provided there are no dues, damage charges or library fines. Refunds are processed through NEFT within 30 working days of the clearance certificate being issued.

## Fee Concessions and Scholarships
Eligible students may get a fee concession or a scholarship. See the Scholarships document for the government and college merit schemes. Fee concession applications are made at the Accounts Office within 30 days of admission.

## Contact
Accounts & Fees Office: accounts@college.edu, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Fee-related queries can also be raised as a ticket under the Fees category.
