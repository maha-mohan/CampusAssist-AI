# College and Class Timings (SAMPLE DATA)

## College Hours
The college works Monday to Saturday. Classes run from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The administrative offices (College Office, Accounts, Admissions, Examination Cell) work from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. All offices are closed on Sundays and government holidays.

## Daily Class Schedule
| Period | Time |
|---|---|
| Period 1 | 9:00 AM – 9:50 AM |
| Period 2 | 9:50 AM – 10:40 AM |
| Tea break | 10:40 AM – 10:55 AM |
| Period 3 | 10:55 AM – 11:45 AM |
| Period 4 | 11:45 AM – 12:35 PM |
| Lunch break | 12:35 PM – 1:25 PM |
| Period 5 | 1:25 PM – 2:15 PM |
| Period 6 | 2:15 PM – 3:05 PM |
| Period 7 | 3:05 PM – 4:00 PM |

Laboratory sessions take two or three continuous periods. Saturday classes follow the same schedule, and Saturday afternoon sessions are used for club activities, mentoring and remedial classes.

## Bus Timings
Morning buses leave the pick-up stops so as to reach the campus by 8:45 AM. Evening buses leave the campus at 4:45 PM.

## Late Arrival
Students arriving after 9:10 AM are marked late. Three late arrivals in a month are counted as one day of absence.
