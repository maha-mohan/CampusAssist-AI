# Scholarships (SAMPLE DATA)

Students can receive scholarships from the government, from the college, or both. Government scholarships follow state norms. College merit scholarships are awarded on Class 12 marks and renewed each year on academic performance.

## Government Scholarships
- **First Graduate Scholarship:** students who are the first graduate in their family receive a tuition fee concession as per Government of Tamil Nadu norms. Apply through the state scholarship portal with the family income certificate.
- **Post-Matric Scholarship for SC / ST / BC / MBC students:** paid through the state scholarship portal. The amount depends on the course and the family income limit set by the state.
- **Pudhumai Penn / Tamil Pudhalvan scheme:** ₹1,000 per month for eligible students who studied in government schools. Eligibility is checked against the school record and the family income limit.

## College Merit Scholarships 2026-27
- Students with 95% and above in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry in Class 12: 50% tuition fee waiver.
- Students with 90% to 94.9% in the same subjects: 25% tuition fee waiver.
- The scholarship is renewed every year if the student maintains a CGPA of 8.0 or above.
- A student whose CGPA falls below 8.0 in a year receives the scholarship for the next year at the reduced rate, subject to the Accounts Office review.

## Other Concessions
- **Sports quota:** students who represent the state or the country in recognised sports can receive a concession of up to 25% on tuition, subject to the sports committee's approval.
- **Children of college staff:** a 30% concession on tuition fees for students who are children of full-time college staff.

## How to Apply
1. Check the eligibility criteria for each scholarship in this document.
2. Collect the application form from the Accounts Office, or download it from the fee portal.
3. Attach the documents: Class 12 mark sheet, community certificate (if applicable), family income certificate, the admission confirmation letter and a copy of the Aadhaar card.
4. Submit the completed form at the Accounts Office within 30 days of admission.
5. Track the status on the fee portal. The Accounts Office announces the list of approved scholarships each semester.

## Important Dates
- College merit scholarship applications: within 30 days of admission.
- Government scholarship applications: as announced on the state scholarship portal, usually between July and October.
- Renewal applications for continuing students: before 30 September each year.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Can I get both a merit scholarship and a government scholarship?** Yes, in most cases. The combined concession cannot exceed the tuition fee for the year.
- **What if my application is late?** Late applications are considered only if the Accounts Office finds a valid reason, and the scholarship is then applied from the next semester.

Apply for scholarships at the Accounts Office within 30 days of admission. For help with the state portal, raise a ticket under the Scholarship category.
