# Hostel Room Types, Mess Menu and Timings (SAMPLE DATA)

Full weekly mess menu, room types and daily timings for the boys' and girls' hostels. Rules and the application process are in the Hostel Information document.

## Room Types and Annual Fees 2026-27
| Room type | Features | Annual fee (including mess) |
|---|---|---|
| 4-sharing (non-AC) | 4 cots, 4 cupboards, ceiling fans, attached bathroom | ₹85,000 |
| 3-sharing (non-AC) | 3 cots, 3 cupboards, ceiling fans, attached bathroom | ₹95,000 |
| 2-sharing (AC) | 2 cots, 2 cupboards, air conditioner, attached bathroom | ₹1,30,000 |
| Single room (AC) | 1 cot, cupboard, study table, air conditioner, attached bathroom | ₹1,60,000 |

The hostel has four room types: 4-sharing non-AC (₹85,000 a year), 3-sharing non-AC (₹95,000 a year), 2-sharing AC (₹1,30,000 a year) and single AC room (₹1,60,000 a year). All fees include the mess.

Hostel fees are payable in two instalments, the same schedule as the tuition fee. A refundable security deposit of ₹5,000 is collected at the time of allotment, and is refunded after the room is inspected when the student leaves the hostel. Single rooms are limited, allotted on merit, and given mainly to final-year and postgraduate students.

## Quick Answers
- **What are the hostel room types?** 4-sharing non-AC, 3-sharing non-AC, 2-sharing AC and single AC room.
- **Are AC rooms available in the hostel?** Yes, AC rooms are available: the 2-sharing AC room costs ₹1,30,000 a year and the single AC room costs ₹1,60,000 a year.
- **What is the fee for a single room in the hostel?** The single AC room costs ₹1,60,000 a year, including mess.
- **What is the hostel fee for a 4-sharing room?** ₹85,000 a year, including mess.
- **What is the hostel in time (in-time) for students?** The hostel in time is 8:30 PM on weekdays and 9:00 PM on weekends.
- **What are the mess timings?** Breakfast 7:30 AM – 8:30 AM, lunch 12:30 PM – 1:45 PM, evening snacks 4:45 PM – 5:30 PM, dinner 7:30 PM – 8:45 PM.
- **Is non-veg food served in the mess?** Yes. Non-vegetarian items are served on selected days, and vegetarian options are always available.
- **When can parents visit the hostel?** On Sundays between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM.

## Laundry and Services
Laundry is collected and returned on alternate days for a monthly charge of ₹300. The medical room is staffed by a nurse every day, and a doctor visits on Tuesdays and Fridays.

## Contact
Hostel Office: hostel@college.edu, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Complaints about food or rooms can be raised as a ticket under the Hostel category.
