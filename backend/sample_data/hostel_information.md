# Hostel Information (SAMPLE DATA)

The college provides separate hostels for boys and girls inside the campus with 24-hour security, CCTV, Wi-Fi, a gym, a reading room and a resident warden. Each hostel block has a warden's office, a common room with a television, and a medical first-aid kit maintained by the college health centre.

## Special Diets
Special diets are available on written request to the warden, with a doctor's note for medical diets.

## Facilities
- Wi-Fi in every room and in the common areas, with a fair-usage limit.
- Laundry service, collected and returned every alternate day for a monthly charge.
- Reading room open until 10:30 PM on weekdays.
- Gym open from 6:00 AM to 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- Medical room staffed by a nurse, with a doctor visit twice a week.

## How to Apply for the Hostel (Admission Process)
1. The college admission must be confirmed first. Hostel forms are issued only after this.
2. Collect the hostel application form from the Hostel Office, fill it in and attach two passport-size photographs.
3. Room allotment is made on merit, based on distance from home, and in the order the forms are received.
4. Pay the first instalment of the hostel fee and the security deposit at the Accounts Office.

## Rules
- In-time for students is 8:30 PM on weekdays and 9:00 PM on weekends.
- Late returns require written permission from the warden. Three late returns in a month result in a warning letter to the parents.
- Hostel visiting hours: visitors are allowed only on Sundays between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM, and must sign the visitors' register at the gate.
- Cooking inside rooms, use of heating appliances and smoking are not allowed.
- Ragging, alcohol and drugs lead to immediate expulsion from the hostel and disciplinary action under the college rules.
- Students leaving the hostel for a vacation must complete the leave form at the Hostel Office at least 2 days in advance.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **How do I apply for a hostel room?** Confirm your college admission first, collect the hostel application form from the Hostel Office, attach two passport-size photographs, and pay the first instalment of the hostel fee and the security deposit at the Accounts Office.
- **Can parents stay overnight?** Parents can stay in the guest room on prior booking with the warden, subject to availability.
- **Is there a hostel for first-year students?** Yes. First-year students are allotted rooms in the boys' or girls' block as per their gender.
- **What happens to the security deposit?** It is refunded in full after the room is inspected for damage and the student has no dues. Deposits are refunded through NEFT within 30 working days of leaving.
