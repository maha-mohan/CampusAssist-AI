# Syllabus Overview (SAMPLE DATA)

The curriculum follows the Anna University regulations 2021, with the college's own value-added courses. Each course has a credit value. The B.E. / B.Tech programme requires about 165 credits in 8 semesters. The full syllabus for each programme is available on the Anna University website and the student ERP portal.

## Other Programmes (core areas)
- **AI&DS:** Statistics for Data Science, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Visualisation, Big Data, Natural Language Processing.
- **IT:** Web Technologies, Mobile Application Development, Information Security, Cloud Services, IoT.
- **ECE:** Electronic Circuits, Signals and Systems, Digital Signal Processing, VLSI Design, Embedded Systems, Wireless Communication.
- **EEE:** Electric Circuits, Electrical Machines, Power Systems, Control Systems, Power Electronics, Renewable Energy.
- **Mechanical:** Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Strength of Materials, Manufacturing Processes, Heat Transfer, CAD/CAM.
- **Civil:** Structural Analysis, Soil Mechanics, Surveying, Concrete Technology, Transportation Engineering, Environmental Engineering.
- **MBA:** Management Theory, Financial Accounting, Marketing Management, HR Management, Business Analytics, Strategic Management.

## Evaluation Pattern
| Component | Weightage |
|---|---|
| Internal assessments (two tests, assignments, attendance) | 40% |
| End-semester examination | 60% |

For laboratory courses, the internal marks are 60% and the end-semester practical exam is 40%. A student must score at least 45% in the end-semester exam and 50% overall to pass.

## Electives, Add-On and Value-Added Courses
- Professional electives are offered from semester 5, in areas such as cybersecurity, cloud, IoT and data analytics.
- Open electives can be chosen from other departments.
- Value-added courses (30 hours each) are offered in the summer: Python for data science, AutoCAD, and communication skills.
- Students can earn up to 8 credits through NPTEL or other approved online courses.

## Internship and Project
- Internship of at least 8 weeks, normally after the third year, is mandatory.
- The final-year project runs in semesters 7 and 8 and is evaluated through reviews and a viva voce.
