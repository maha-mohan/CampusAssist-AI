# Attendance and Leave Policy (SAMPLE DATA)

## Attendance Requirement
- Students must have at least **75%** attendance in each semester, counted across all theory and laboratory subjects.
- Attendance is marked every period and is visible on the student ERP portal. Parents receive a monthly attendance SMS.
- Students with less than 75% attendance cannot receive the hall ticket for the end-semester examination.

## Condonation of Shortage
| Attendance | Action |
|---|---|
| 75% and above | Eligible for the hall ticket |
| 65% to 74% | Can apply for condonation with a medical or other valid reason; a condonation fee of ₹500 applies |
| Below 65% | Not eligible; the student must repeat the semester |

Condonation is allowed only once in the programme for first-year students, and up to two times in the whole programme for others. The application goes to the HOD and then the Examination Cell.

## Types of Leave
- **Casual leave:** up to 3 days in a semester, with the class mentor's approval.
- **Medical leave:** supported by a medical certificate from a registered doctor, submitted within 3 days of returning.
- **On-duty (OD) leave:** for approved college events, sports meets, competitions and placement drives. The OD slip must be signed by the staff in charge and the HOD.
- **Hostel leave (home visit):** apply to the warden, with the parents' consent by phone for first-year students.

OD and medical leave days are counted as present for attendance up to a maximum of 10% of working days.

## How to Apply for Leave
1. Fill in the leave application form on the student ERP portal, or take a printed form from the department office.
2. Attach the medical certificate or OD slip, if applicable.
3. Get the signature of the class mentor and submit it to the HOD.
4. Approved leave is updated in the attendance records within 2 working days.

## Late Arrival and Early Departure
Students arriving after 9:10 AM are marked late. Leaving the campus early needs a gate pass signed by the class mentor, and day scholars may leave only with the HOD's permission.

## Long Absence
A student absent for more than 15 continuous days without leave is reported to the parents, and the name may be removed from the rolls after 30 days.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Is attendance counted on Saturdays?** Yes, Saturdays are working days.
- **Do internships count as attendance?** Approved internships during the semester are counted as OD leave.
- **Who do I contact about an attendance error?** Your class mentor, within 7 days, or raise a ticket under the Academics category.
