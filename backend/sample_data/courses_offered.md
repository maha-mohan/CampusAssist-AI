# Courses and Departments (SAMPLE DATA)

ABC Engineering College is affiliated to Anna University, Chennai and approved by AICTE. Undergraduate programmes run for four years, and postgraduate programmes for two years. Each programme follows the Anna University curriculum, with added industry-oriented electives, labs and project work.

| Programme | Seats | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|

## What Students Study
- **Years 1 and 2:** foundation courses in mathematics, physics, chemistry, programming, engineering drawing and communication skills.
- **Years 3 and 4:** core subjects of the discipline, electives, industry projects and a final-year project.
- **Internships:** a mandatory internship of at least 8 weeks, usually in the summer after the third year.

## Departments and Facilities
Each department has modern laboratories, a department library and industry-supported centres of excellence.

- **Computer Science and Engineering:** software labs, a cloud and DevOps lab, and a centre of excellence in cybersecurity.
- **Artificial Intelligence and Data Science:** GPU-enabled machine learning lab and a data analytics studio.
- **Information Technology:** networking lab, web and mobile development studio.
- **Electronics and Communication Engineering:** embedded systems lab, VLSI design lab and a signal processing lab.
- **Electrical and Electronics Engineering:** power electronics lab, electric machines lab and renewable energy lab.
- **Mechanical Engineering:** CAD/CAM lab, thermal engineering lab and a fabrication workshop.
- **Civil Engineering:** structural engineering lab, surveying field and a materials testing lab.
- **MBA:** case-based teaching rooms, a finance and analytics lab and a business incubation cell.

## Academic Support
- Remedial classes for students who need extra help in first-year mathematics and programming.
- Mentoring: each student is assigned a faculty mentor from the first semester.
- Central library open Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM, with digital journal access 24x7 from the campus network.
